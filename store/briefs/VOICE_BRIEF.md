# Voice recording brief (give this to the voice actor)

**Project:** Angel Artic, a speech-sound practice app for children aged 3 to 8.
**What we need:** one friendly, clear voice reading every line in `voice-script.csv`.
**Size:** 2,048 short clips, about 80 minutes of finished audio (732 of the clips are a slower version of the same word).

## The voice
- Warm, patient, smiling. Like a kind nursery or primary-school teacher. Never shouty, never babyish.
- Clear and slightly slower than normal speech. Every sound crisp, because children copy what they hear.
- One accent for the whole project. Tell us which (British or American). Keep it identical from the first clip to the last.
- Same microphone, same room, same distance for every session, so clips sound like one person.

## How to read each line
- **normal**: read naturally and clearly, as if saying it to a four-year-old.
- **slow**: the same word, stretched gently so each sound is easy to hear (for example "s-u-n" blended, not spelled). About 1.5 times slower. Do not add pauses between letters.
- **Words**: say only the word. No "the", no extra breath words.
- **Sentences, phrases, stories**: friendly and expressive, with natural pauses at full stops.
- **Syllables** (`pah`, `ee-pee`): say them smoothly as one sound, never spelled out letter by letter.
- **Sounds that need care:** `th` as in "thumb" (voiceless) versus `th` as in "this" (voiced), /zh/ as in "vision", /ng/ as in "ring". If a line is unclear, ask before recording.

## Technical
- WAV, 44.1 or 48 kHz, 24 bit if possible, mono.
- Quiet room, no echo, pop filter. No background noise, no clicks, no mouth noise.
- Trim to about 0.1 s of silence at the start and end. Do not add effects, music or heavy compression.
- Keep the loudness the same across clips. We also level the volume ourselves.
- Do not normalise every clip to the same peak by hand; leave natural differences between a short word and a sentence.

## File names (very important)
Name each file exactly like the `file` column in `voice-script.csv`, but with `.wav`:
- `pig.wav`  (normal)   and   `pig__slow.wav`  (slow, two underscores)
- Spaces become underscores: `ice_cream.wav`
- Sentences use the whole sentence in lower case with underscores: `the_pig_is_pink.wav`

Send the files in one folder (zip is fine). We check them automatically; any wrongly named file is reported back to you.

## Delivery in stages (recommended)
1. **Test batch (30 clips):** 10 words, 10 slow words, 5 sentences, 5 syllables. We approve the voice before you record the rest.
2. **Words and slow words**, then **sentences and phrases**, then **stories and Talk words**.

## Rights
We need a buyout (all rights, unlimited use in the app, the website and adverts, for ever) and written permission to use the voice in the app. Please confirm this in your quote.

## What to quote
Price per finished minute or per clip, plus one round of re-takes for mispronunciations.
