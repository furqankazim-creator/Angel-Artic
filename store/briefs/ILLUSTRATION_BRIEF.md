# Illustration brief (give this to the illustrator)

**Project:** Angel Artic, a speech-sound practice app for children aged 3 to 8, and its picture-board companion Angel Talk.
**What we need:** 458 single illustrations, listed in `illustration-list.csv`, all in one consistent style.

## Style
- Friendly, flat, rounded shapes. Soft outlines or none. Think quality picture books, not clip art.
- Bright but calm colours. Keep a warm, gentle palette that works on a cream background (#FFF9F0). Our colours: violet #6C5CE7, coral #FF6B6B, sunny yellow #FFC93C, teal #00B8A9, sky blue #4D96FF, ink #2D2A4A.
- One clear subject per picture, centred, with a little space around it. No text, no letters, no logos.
- Simple enough to understand in one second, at small sizes on a phone.
- Same line weight, same shading style, same level of detail in all 458.
- Child safe: no scary faces, no violence, no brand characters. Diverse, friendly people (different skin tones and hair, some with glasses or hearing aids).

## Why the picture matters
A child must recognise the word from the picture alone. A confusing picture teaches the wrong word. So:
- Draw the **everyday, most common version** of the object.
- Do not hide the sound the child is practising. For "sun", show a sun, not a beach scene.
- For actions (run, jump), show a child doing it clearly.
- Rows marked **abstract** in the sheet (this, that, more, please, stop, together, mistake...) cannot be a single object. Please propose a simple scene or gesture, and show us a sketch before finishing.

## Technical
- PNG, transparent background, 1024 x 1024 px (we scale down to 512).
- Safe margin of about 8 percent on every side.
- Name each file exactly like the `file_name` column: `pig.png`, `ice_cream.png`. Lower case, underscores.
- Also send the layered source files (Illustrator, Affinity, Procreate or Figma) so we can change colours later.

## Delivery in stages (recommended)
1. **Style test (12 pictures):** pig, sun, ball, cat, house, fish, baby, banana, car, tree, hand, shoe. We approve the style first.
2. Then the objects in sound order, then the abstract words last.

## Rights
Full buyout with the right to use, change and publish in the app, the stores, the website and adverts, for ever. Please confirm in your quote.

## What to quote
A price per illustration at 458 pictures, with a lower rate for simple objects, and one round of changes per batch.
