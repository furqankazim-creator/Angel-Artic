Angel Artic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. Draft: have a lawyer review it before publishing. Fill in the postal address at the end.

Angel Artic is a speech sound practice app for children, used with a parent or carer. This policy explains what the app does with information. Publisher: Hope Bridge Digital. Contact: hello@hopebridgedigital.com.

The short version

  • Everything the app saves stays on your device.
  • We do not run ads. We do not use advertising or analytics tools.
  • We do not have child accounts, chat, or social features.
  • We do not sell or share information.

What the app stores on your device

  • The child's first name (or nickname), age band, chosen sounds and daily goal.
  • Practice results: scores, stars, streaks and stickers.
  • Voice recordings made during practice, and optional parent-added photos and voice recordings for custom words.
  • Parent settings, including a PIN and recovery word.
  • A short problem note if the app ever crashes. It holds only the error text, not names, scores or recordings, and stays on the device until a parent chooses to share it.

This information is not sent to us. A parent can delete a child's data at any time in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone permission so the child can say practice words. Recording starts only when a button is pressed. The app measures loudness on the device to check that the child spoke.

Optional online pronunciation scoring

This is off by default. By default the app scores practice on the device and nothing is uploaded.

A parent can turn on better scoring in Settings in one of two ways. (1) Enter their own Microsoft Azure Speech key: the recording of that one practice word is then sent to Microsoft's service to be scored, under Microsoft's privacy terms. (2) Enter the address of a scoring server run by the app's publisher or by the parent: the recording of that one word is sent to that server, which may convert it to a standard audio format and pass it to Microsoft Azure to be scored. The server does not keep the audio, does not keep a name, and deletes the file straight after scoring. Only the word being practised and the audio are sent. Nothing is sent unless a parent has switched this on.

Backups and sharing

Reports and backups are created only when a parent taps Share, and go wherever the parent chooses. A backup file does not include the PIN, recovery word, keys or recordings.

Voices

The app plays recorded or device-generated voices. The Talk screen uses the voices that are already built into your phone. No audio is sent anywhere for this.

Links to Angel Talk

The app has an information page about Angel Talk, a separate communication app from the same publisher. Buttons on that page open the Angel Talk website or your email app so a parent can ask about it. These buttons first ask a grown-up to type a number written in words. Nothing from the app is sent when you tap them. The website and your email app have their own policies.

Purchases

The app is free. It has no purchases, subscriptions or ads.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children with a parent or carer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parent areas are protected by a PIN, and links that leave the app are protected by a grown-up check. A lawyer should confirm the wording for COPPA (United States) and GDPR-K (Europe) before publishing.

Not medical advice

Angel Artic is a practice tool. It is not a diagnosis and does not replace a speech-language pathologist or other professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the app will link to the new version.
