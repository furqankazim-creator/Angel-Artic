## Long description
Angel Artic helps children aged 3 to 8 practise speech sounds in a calm, playful way.

• 25 English sounds, practised at the start, middle and end of words
• Content that grows with your child: gentle words for ages 3 to 4, longer words and full sentences for 5 to 6, tongue twisters and stories for 7 to 8
• A clear ladder from easy to hard: the sound, syllables, words, phrases, sentences, then a short story
• Watch-the-mouth animations show where the tongue and lips go for every sound
• A clear voice says each word, with a slow option
• Stars, stickers, a daily mission and an adventure map keep practice fun
• Angel Talk board: tap pictures to build a sentence, with over 100 words
• Parents: progress for every sound, a weekly PDF report to share with your therapist, and custom words with your own photos and voice
• Completely free: no purchases, no subscription
• Works offline, and available in English, Arabic, Urdu and Spanish screens

Built to be gentle: silence never earns a score, and a child can never lose progress.
No ads. No chat. No child accounts. Recordings and scores stay on your device.

Angel Artic is a practice tool. It is not a diagnosis and does not replace a speech-language pathologist.
