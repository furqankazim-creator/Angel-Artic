## 1. 😇 Angel Artic — Gentle Speech Sound Practice

> **Tagline:** *Speak • Play • Shine — Articulation practice built for children.*  
> **Category:** Speech Sound Practice / Articulation Therapy  
> **Target Audience:** Children with speech sound delays, articulation challenges, childhood apraxia of speech, or autism, supported by parents and Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs).  
> **Core Philosophy:** *"Silence never earns a score, and a child never loses progress."*

<div align="center">

[![Scan to open Angel Artic on Website](https://api.qrserver.com/v1/create-qr-code/?size=200x200&margin=10&data=https%3A%2F%2Fhopebirdgedigital.site%2F)](https://hopebirdgedigital.site/)

**[📲 Scan or Tap to View Angel Artic on Live Site: https://hopebirdgedigital.site/](https://hopebirdgedigital.site/)**

</div>

### 🌟 What It Does
Angel Artic helps children master speech sound production through a structured, encouraging experience. Children practice phonemes in three distinct word positions (**Initial**, **Medial**, and **Final**) with clear auditory models and visual animations.

### 🚀 Key Features & Innovations
* **🗣️ 17 Speech Sounds & Word Decks:** Full coverage from early development sounds (`/p/`, `/b/`, `/m/`) to advanced phonemes (`/r/`, `/s/`, `/th/`, `/ch/`).
* **🎙️ Microphone Voice-Gate Algorithm:** Distinguishes between ambient room noise, silence, and genuine vocal effort. No stars are given for random screen tapping — real voice input is required.
* **👄 Visual Mouth Placement Guides:** Step-by-step animations showing where to place the tongue, teeth, and lips to produce tricky sounds accurately.
* **📷 Custom Words & Photos:** Parents can upload family photos and record their own voices for personalized practice.
* **🔒 100% Private & Offline:** Zero third-party ads, zero tracking scripts. All voice recordings stay strictly on the device.

## 2. 💬 Angel Talk — AAC Picture Communication

> **Tagline:** *A voice for every child.*  
> **Category:** Augmentative & Alternative Communication (AAC)  
> **Target Audience:** Non-verbal or minimally verbal children and teens, individuals with autism (ASD), Down syndrome, apraxia, cerebral palsy, or speech delay.  
> **Core Philosophy:** *"Angel Artic is for saying it clearly — Angel Talk is for saying it right now."*

<div align="center">

[![Scan to open Angel Talk on Website](https://api.qrserver.com/v1/create-qr-code/?size=200x200&margin=10&data=https%3A%2F%2Fhopebirdgedigital.site%2Fapps%2Fangel-talk)](https://hopebirdgedigital.site/apps/angel-talk)

**[📲 Scan or Tap to View Angel Talk on Live Site: https://hopebirdgedigital.site/apps/angel-talk](https://hopebirdgedigital.site/apps/angel-talk)**

</div>

### 🌟 What It Does
Angel Talk transforms picture taps into fluent, spoken speech. Children tap symbol tiles to communicate immediate needs, build complex sentence strips, or engage in social conversations using natural speech synthesis.

### 🚀 Key Features & Innovations
* **🖼️ 14,800+ Symbol Library:** Comprehensive, high-contrast symbol tiles categorized by Food, Feelings, Actions, People, Daily Needs, School, and Places.
* **🌍 Bilingual High-Definition Natural Voices:** Natural, expressive child and adult voices in both **English and Arabic**.
* **🎙️ Voice Add (Rapid Board Creation):** Allows parents, educators, and therapists to add new picture tiles simply by speaking the word aloud.
* **📷 Custom Photo Support:** Snap and attach real photos of family members, preferred snacks, personal toys, and daily environments with custom voice recordings.
* **🧘 Sensory & Routine Tools:** Visual schedules, social stories, and an interactive calming breathing pacer.

## 3. 🌸 BloomLearn — Visual Language & Concept Learning

> **Tagline:** *Say it. See it. Learn it.*  
> **Category:** Visual Language, Comprehension & Concept Learning  
> **Target Audience:** Autistic children, visual learners, and children developing language comprehension, grammar, and early math concepts.

<div align="center">

[![Scan to open BloomLearn on Website](https://api.qrserver.com/v1/create-qr-code/?size=200x200&margin=10&data=https%3A%2F%2Fhopebirdgedigital.site%2F)](https://hopebirdgedigital.site/)

### 🌟 What It Does
BloomLearn is an interactive AI-powered learning platform that transforms words, sentences, and mathematical concepts into visual experiences. Children can speak or type a sentence and instantly see its meaning illustrated on screen, or engage in structured video-based learning for Grades 1–5 in English, Mathematics, and Science.

### 🚀 Core Features
1. **🎨 AI Text-to-Visual Learning:** Every typed or spoken sentence is transformed into an instant visual illustration.
2. **📍 Interactive Spatial Learning:** Understand positions such as *under, above, behind, beside, and between* through interactive visual scenes.
3. **🔢 Visual Mathematics:** Learn numbers, calculations, shapes, and mathematical story problems through pictures and animations.
4. **🎬 Video-Based Learning:** Watch educational videos organized by topic, subject, and grade level. Each video explains concepts step by step using engaging visuals and animations.
5. **📚 Grade-Based Curriculum (Grades 1–5):** Structured learning content covering:
   * **English**
   * **Mathematics**
   * **Science**
6. **📝 Topic-Wise Lessons & Quizzes:** Each subject contains organized chapters, topics, explanatory videos, visual examples, and practice activities.

### 🔄 Video Learning Workflow
1. **Select Grade:** Choose Grade 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
2. **Choose Subject:** English, Mathematics, or Science.
3. **Select Topic:** Browse chapters and individual learning topics.
4. **Watch Educational Video:** Learn through animated explanations, illustrations, and examples.
5. **Practice and Test:** Complete interactive exercises, quizzes, and topic-based questions.
6. **Track Progress:** Monitor completed videos, quiz scores, and learning progress.

## 🛡️ Child Safety, Privacy & Compliance Standards

All software produced by **Hope Bridge Digital** complies with the highest international privacy and family safety standards:
